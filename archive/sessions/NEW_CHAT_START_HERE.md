## Why This Works

**PROJECT_STATUS.md contains**:
- ✅ What's complete vs in progress
- ✅ Test status (passing/failing)
- ✅ Known blockers and issues
- ✅ File locations and architecture
- ✅ What we were working on last
- ✅ Immediate next steps
- ✅ Quick commands and references

**Result**: AI gets full context in 30 seconds vs 10 minutes of Q&A.

## Quick Tips

### DO ✅
- Reference PROJECT_STATUS.md explicitly
- Mention specific task if known
- Note any recent discoveries
- Ask AI to confirm they've read the status

### DON'T ❌
- Assume AI remembers previous chats
- Skip reading PROJECT_STATUS.md
- Be vague ("continue the work")
- Forget to mention blockers
