QUESTIONS ANSWERED
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: Where is bundle tree stored?
A: In .log-scout/bundles/ directory
   - index.json (master list)
   - bundle_{id}/bundle.json (metadata)
   - bundle_{id}/logs/ (extracted files)

Q: What happens when deleting bundles?
A: Complete cleanup:
   1. Remove workspace folder reference
   2. Delete physical files
   3. Update index.json
   4. Refresh tree view

Q: What's the end result?
A: Clean workspace with no zombie folders or orphaned references
